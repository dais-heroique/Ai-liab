FAQ

Built for autonomous operations.

Does Arexid replace our AI models?

No. Arexid is model-agnostic infrastructure around existing agents, models and tools.

Can another AI verify an agent?

Yes. High-impact actions can be routed to independent specialist verifiers. The verifier is advisory; deterministic policies remain authoritative.

What if a verifier says an action is unsafe?

Arexid can block the action, request a safer alternative, or re-evaluate after the agent proposes a modified action.

Can this run without humans in the loop?

Yes. Arexid is designed for automated governance. Escalation can exist as a configurable fallback, but autonomous enforcement does not require a human for every decision.
