FAQ

Questions teams ask before deployment.

Does Arexid replace our AI models?

No. Arexid is a control layer around existing agents, models and tools.

What happens when an action is risky?

Policies and risk controls can produce ALLOW, HUMAN_REVIEW or BLOCK, depending on your configuration.

Can engineers integrate it into existing systems?

Yes. The platform exposes evaluation APIs and SDK-oriented integration paths so enforcement can sit at the action boundary.

Does Arexid guarantee legal protection?

No. It provides governance, prevention, evidence and human oversight. It does not make an absolute legal guarantee about liability.
