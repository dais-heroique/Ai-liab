FAQ

Questions enterprise teams ask.

What does the Gateway control?

It evaluates an agent action before execution and returns an operational decision such as ALLOW, HUMAN_REVIEW or BLOCK based on configured controls.

Does it replace our AI models?

No. The Gateway is designed as a control layer around your existing agents, models and tools.

What evidence is recorded?

Depending on the configured flow, decision context, policy outcomes, audit identifiers and cryptographic proof can be recorded for later investigation.

Does it guarantee legal protection?

No. It provides governance, prevention, evidence and human oversight. It does not make an absolute legal guarantee about liability.
